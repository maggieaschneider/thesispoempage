two

it’s over

done it’s

ice cream after dinner and sleep

overs accompanied by welcomed blindness

it’s painting my nails again

moth to flame to pages burnt

i crave more what is yet to come than i long for the ache of the past

morning again and

it’s the joy of a new day that holds my breath and suspends my foot before i step out of the door

it’s the crisp morning air

it’s the lines i follow carefully

pen scratches

path of grass

it’s the path i know well

trees fall silent

drifting course,

like unwanted emotion

but is any emotion really unwanted if it’s all- and only- my own?

it’s exposing myself

lying to say i could be honestly hopeful

dressing up just to be taken apart again

lured out of my house just for it to never be

all mine again

it’s awaiting a phone call

i’d rather forget

the day is done there’s nothing more to do

there are no more

words to say

it’s every single crevice buried in my hand

it’s every single thought tossed from my mind

wind blows

pages before i’ve finished writing on them

before my hand can finish

it’s attempt of giving myself life

it’s wake up and breathe

it’s continue on

it’s don’t stop

it’s fall asleep and breathe again

buildings building building

blocks of landscapes i make permanent in memory

artist’s paintbrush,

my sky

it’s inability to find my favorite

lie of reassurance

it’s hating the maze of maybe

it’s hating the promise of perhaps

it’s unraveling yourself on purpose because you’ve lost sense of the word.
